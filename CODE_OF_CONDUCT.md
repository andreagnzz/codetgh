# Code of conduct

CodeTgh is built in the open by people with different backgrounds and levels of experience. Everyone taking part in issues, pull requests and discussions agrees to:

- be respectful and assume good faith;
- give and accept feedback on the work, never on the person;
- keep it free of harassment, insults, discrimination and sexual content;
- respect privacy: never share someone's personal data, tokens or private messages.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and block participants who do not follow these rules.

To report a problem privately, use the Security tab's **Report a vulnerability** form and say it is a conduct report, or contact a maintainer listed in `.github/CODEOWNERS`. Reports are handled confidentially.
